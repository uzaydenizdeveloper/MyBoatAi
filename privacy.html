The data controller for the purposes of KVKK and, where applicable, GDPR is uzaydenizlabs, Türkiye. Contact: support@myboatai.com. 3. What Data We Collect and Why

Most data stays on your device. External services receive data only when you actively use a feature that requires it. Account data: Email address, sign-in method and a user ID, processed by Firebase Authentication. Legal basis: contract performance. Location: GPS coordinates when you grant permission, and destinations and routes you choose. Used to display position, plan routes and retrieve weather and dining information. Routes, trip history and positions are stored on your device only. Legal basis: your consent, withdrawable at any time through device settings. Boat data, maintenance logs, documents, photos and settings: Stored on your device only. We do not upload or retain this data on our servers. Voice input: Your device converts speech to text locally. We do not record or store audio. The resulting text may be sent to Google Gemini for maintenance and engine-hour entries. Legal basis: your consent. Camera and photos: Used only when you add a photo or document. They stay on your device unless you attach one to feedback. Feedback: Message text, your email address and any attachment are sent to us by email. Legal basis: legitimate interest. Acceptance records: For each legal document you accept we record the document version, acceptance date, your email address and account ID. Retained for 10 years after account deletion as required by law. Technical data: Hosting provider and third-party services listed in Section 5 receive standard technical data such as IP address, request time and requested resource. Place suggestions (optional): If you tick "Help add this place to our global map", the place name, type, coordinates and date are emailed to us without your email or account ID. Please do not include personal information in a place name.

4. Local vs. Cloud Data

MyBoatAI is built on a local-first principle. The following data never leaves your device: boat profiles, maintenance logs, trip history, routes, fishing spots, saved places, documents, licences, photos, checklists, settings and downloaded map packages. The following data is sent to external services when you use the relevant feature: account credentials (Firebase), weather forecasts (Open-Meteo), nearby restaurant suggestions (Google Places via our server), AI assistant queries (Google Gemini), map tiles and region files (Cloudflare / Protomaps), navigational warnings (compiled from official maritime authority publications, served via Cloudflare), map rendering (Leaflet / OpenSeaMap).

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Each provider receives only the data strictly necessary for its function. Google Firebase / Google Cloud: Account sign-in, acceptance records and server hosting. Receives: email address, account ID, acceptance records and technical logs including IP address. Cloudflare: Map tiles, region packages and warning files from data.myboatai.com. Receives: IP address and requested file names. No account data. Protomaps: Map tile packages are built using Protomaps and served via Cloudflare. Protomaps does not receive your data at runtime; tile requests go to Cloudflare as described above. Leaflet / OpenSeaMap: Used to render the map within the app. Map tiles may be loaded from OpenSeaMap tile servers, which receive your IP address and the tile coordinates requested. No account data is shared. Open-Meteo: Weather and marine forecasts. Receives: route or destination coordinates and IP address. No account data. Google Places: Nearby restaurant and cafe suggestions. Our server sends only destination coordinates; your IP address and account data are not passed to Google. Google's Privacy Policy applies. Google Gemini API: Powers AI features. Only data required for the specific feature is sent; email and account ID are never transmitted. We use the free tier — Google may use submitted content to improve its models. Do not include sensitive personal information in text you send to the AI assistant. Resend: Transactional emails and place-suggestion forwarding. Receives: email address and message content. Place suggestions sent without account ID. Unsplash: Destination photos. Receives: IP address. Google Fonts: App fonts. Receives: IP address. GitHub Pages: Legal documents and version files. Receives: IP address. Device or browser speech service: Converts voice to text under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store audio. Google AdMob: Google AdMob: Displays advertisements within the app. The SDK may collect your advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options in the app. You can also opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google’s advertising policies apply. We do not receive your advertising ID; we only receive aggregated reporting.

5a. Public Marine Data Sources

Navigational warnings are collected automatically from the official public publications of maritime authorities in Türkiye, Finland and Greece, then delivered via Cloudflare. The app does not contact these authorities directly. These warnings are informational only and do not constitute official navigational data.
