MyBoatAI is operated by uzaydenizlabs. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Retention periods are determined according to uzaydenizlabs's operational requirements and applicable legal obligations. We do not maintain data beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

We implement standard industry security practices, including encryption in transit and secure database rules, to protect your data. No system is fully secure.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this policy; continued use constitutes acceptance. Major changes will be notified within the app.
