MyBoatAI Privacy Policy

1. Who Operates MyBoatAI

MyBoatAI is operated by uzaydenizlabs, an individual developer based in Türkiye. Contact: uzaydeniz@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Account data: email address, sign-in method and a user ID, processed by Firebase Authentication to create and secure your account. Location: your precise GPS location when you allow it, and the destinations and routes you choose. We use it to show your position, plan routes and get weather and nearby-dining information. Your routes, trip history and positions are kept on your device. Boat data, maintenance logs, documents, photos and settings: stored on your device (browser storage). We do not keep them on our servers. Voice: if you use voice input, your browser or operating system speech recognition converts your voice to text. We do not record, receive or store audio. The text may be sent to Google Gemini for maintenance and engine-hour entries, as described below. Camera and photos: used only when you add a photo or document. They stay on your device unless you attach them to feedback. Feedback: if you send feedback, the text, your email address and any attachment you add are sent to us by email. Acceptance records: for each legal document you accept, we store its version, the date, your email address and your account ID. Technical data: our hosting provider and the services listed below receive technical data such as your IP address, the time and the requested address when you use the app. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

3. Local vs. Cloud Data

MyBoatAI operates on a local-first principle. Many settings and records remain on your device (LocalStorage). Data is sent to external services only when you actively use features requiring cloud processing.

4. Service Providers Who Receive Data

Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, database, hosting): email address, account ID, acceptance records and technical logs. Google Gemini API: safety briefings (weather values and boat details), maintenance and engine-hour entries (the text you type or dictate) and nearby dining search (destination name and coordinates). We do not send your email address or account ID. We use the free tier of the Gemini API, and Google's terms for the free tier may allow Google to use submitted content to improve its products. Please do not include sensitive personal information in dictated text. Google Maps Platform: map display and place search. Google receives your IP address and the map area you view. Open-Meteo: weather and marine forecasts. It receives the exact coordinates you request and your IP address. Unsplash: destination photos load from its servers, which receive your IP address. Google Fonts: fonts load from Google servers, which receive your IP address. Resend: delivery of welcome, security and feedback emails (email address and message). Your browser or device speech service: converts voice to text under its own privacy policy.

5. Data Usage Purposes

Data is used to provide requested features (e.g., AI chat, safety briefings, maintenance logs, email communication). We do not sell user data for advertising.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings, or through the account deletion page linked in our Google Play listing. We delete your sign-in account and your email-status record. Data stored on your device is removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. We keep the acceptance records described in section 2 (including email address and account ID) for the period in section 7.

7. Retention

Data on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app. Your account: until you delete it. Acceptance records: for the period required by applicable law. Feedback emails: as long as needed to handle your request and for the period required by applicable law. Technical logs: kept by our hosting provider for a limited period. They do not contain message content.

8. Security

We implement standard industry security practices, including encryption in transit and secure database rules, to protect your data. No system is fully secure.

9. Children's Privacy

MyBoatAI is not intended for users under the age of 18.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this policy; continued use constitutes acceptance. Major changes will be notified within the app.
