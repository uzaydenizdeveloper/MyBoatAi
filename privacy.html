MyBoatAI Privacy Policy

Version: 2.5.0 | Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Who Operates MyBoatAI

MyBoatAI is operated by uzaydenizlabs, an individual developer based in Türkiye. Contact: uzaydeniz@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

Most of your data stays on your device. Data is sent to external services only when you use a feature that needs it (see section 4). Account data: email address, sign-in method and a user ID, processed by Firebase Authentication to create and secure your account. Location: your precise GPS location when you allow it, and the destinations and routes you choose. We use it to show your position, plan routes and get weather and nearby-dining information. Your routes, trip history and positions are kept on your device. Boat data, maintenance logs, documents, photos and settings: stored on your device (browser storage). We do not keep them on our servers. Voice: if you use voice input, your browser or operating system speech recognition converts your voice to text. We do not record, receive or store audio. The text may be sent to Google Gemini for maintenance and engine-hour entries, as described below. Camera and photos: used only when you add a photo or document. They stay on your device unless you attach them to feedback. Feedback: if you send feedback, the text, your email address and any attachment you add are sent to us by email. Acceptance records: for each legal document you accept, we store its version, the date, your email address and your account ID. Technical data: our hosting provider and the services listed below receive technical data such as your IP address, the time and the requested address when you use the app. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

3. Local vs. Cloud Data

MyBoatAI operates on a local-first principle. Many settings and records remain on your device (LocalStorage). Data is sent to external services only when you actively use features requiring cloud processing.

4. Service Providers Who Receive Data

Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, database, hosting): email address, account ID, acceptance records and technical logs. Google Gemini API: safety briefings (weather values and boat details), maintenance and engine-hour entries (the text you type or dictate) and nearby dining search (destination name and coordinates). We do not send your email address or account ID. We use the free tier of the Gemini API, and Google's terms for the free tier may allow Google to use submitted content to improve its products. Please do not include sensitive personal information in dictated text. Google Maps Platform: map display and place search. Google receives your IP address, the map area you view, and any place names you search for. Open-Meteo: weather and marine forecasts. It receives the exact coordinates you request and your IP address. Unsplash: destination photos load from its servers, which receive your IP address. Google Fonts: fonts load from Google servers, which receive your IP address. Resend: delivery of welcome, security and feedback emails (email address and message). Your browser or device speech service: converts voice to text under its own privacy policy.

5. Data Usage Purposes

Data is used to provide the features you use (AI chat, safety briefings, maintenance logs, dining search, feedback and account emails), to keep the service secure, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell user data for advertising.

6. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings, or through the account deletion page linked in our Google Play listing. Deletion is processed immediately: we delete your sign-in account, your welcome-email status record, and the data stored on your device for your account (boats, documents, maintenance records, settings, chats and fishing spots). We keep the acceptance records described in section 2 (including email address and account ID) for the period in section 7.

7. Retention

Data on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app. Your account: until you delete it. Acceptance records: 10 years after account deletion, as required by applicable law and to show which terms applied at the time. Feedback emails: 12 months after your request is handled. Server logs: 30 days. They do not contain message content.

8. Security

We implement standard industry security practices, including encryption in transit and secure database rules, to protect your data. No system is fully secure.

9. Children's Privacy

MyBoatAI is not intended for users under the age of 18.

10. Policy Changes

We may update this policy; continued use constitutes acceptance. Major changes will be notified within the app.

11. Contact

For inquiries, contact: uzaydeniz@gmail.com. Governing law: Republic of Türkiye / Turkish law. Last updated: October 25, 2026.

12. Your Rights, Legal Basis and Transfers

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability of your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time by writing to uzaydeniz@gmail.com. You may complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Board). We process data to provide the service you request, based on your consent for location, camera and microphone, to meet legal obligations for acceptance records, and for security. Our service providers may process data outside your country, including outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area, under their standard safeguards.

Voce: se utilizzi l'input vocale, il riconoscimento vocale del browser o del sistema operativo converte la tua voce in testo. Non registriamo, riceviamo né archiviamo audio. Il testo può essere inviato a Google Gemini per i registri di manutenzione e ore motore, come descritto di seguito. Fotocamera e foto: utilizzate solo quando aggiungi una foto o un documento. Rimangono sul tuo dispositivo a meno che non le alleghi a un feedback. Feedback: se invii un feedback, il testo, il tuo indirizzo email e qualsiasi allegato vengono inviati tramite email. Registri di accettazione: per ogni documento legale accettato, archiviamo la versione, la data, l'indirizzo email e l'ID del tuo account.
