5. Routes and Distances

6. Official Warnings, Weather and Times

Navigational warnings are read automatically from the official public publications of maritime authorities and matched to your route. A warning may be missed or misread, and some warnings are broadcast only by NAVTEX radio and are not published online. Always check official sources, NAVTEX and VHF channel 16 before and during your passage. Weather information is a forecast and may change without notice. Trip duration, arrival time and passing times are calculated at a constant speed and do not account for wind, waves or currents. Times are displayed in the local time of the place concerned.
